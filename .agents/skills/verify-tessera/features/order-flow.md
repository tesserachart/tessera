# Order flow

The Scalp preset puts a 1m order-flow chart (footprint, session volume profile, big trades, CVD) beside a DOM and a time & sales. Zoomed in, the footprint shows each bar's bid and ask figures, and nothing else on the chart may sit on them. Zooming out, the figures step down from 11.5 px to 10.5 px and then 9.5 px as the widest one stops fitting its bar, each step held 4 px of bar spacing so it does not flicker (`of-figure-sizes`). A little further out, while the figures no longer fit the cells, each row still reads as a split of a bid bar and an ask bar in their sides' colours, with the imbalances and POC marks kept; only when the cells themselves can no longer fit does it fall back to plain candles with the POC dashes. The DOM names its columns.

## Sub-features

- `of-legend` autoscaled bars start below the legend and its study rows (Footprint, VP, Big trades).
- `of-rail` no bar is drawn under the drawing rail, or under its tab once the rail is hidden.
- `of-footprint-modes` the footprint's settings `Show` `Bid × Ask` (the default), `Delta`, `Volume`, `Trades` (the count of prints at each price, from the same footprint read: `market.get_footprint` cells are `[price, bid, ask, trades]`) or `Bid × Ask histogram` (each row's bid and ask volume as bars out from the bar's centre, bid left in the bid colour and ask right in the ask colour, the bid × ask figures over them once they fit, no cell tint). `Dim below %` (`Off` by default) fades every row that traded less than that share of its bar's busiest row: its figures go muted and lose their imbalance colour, its tint goes. `Max level` (`Off` by default) outlines each bar's row with the most `Volume`, `Trades`, `Bid` or `Ask` in a 1.5 px mist rule, in place of the thinner POC box on that row. All of them draw on every timeframe, 1D included, with nothing in the legend row.
- `of-profile` while the bars are footprints, the session profile sits in a gutter of its own between the rail and the bars, below the legend. Over candles it draws over the session's first bars.
- `of-profile-periods` every volume profile is the one `volume_profile` study: its `Period` picks `Session`, `Day`, `Week`, `Month`, `Composite`, `Fixed range`, `Anchored` or `Visible` on the instrument's trading calendar, all read through one profile engine (one feed per instrument in the app, `market.get_volume_profile` in the runtime). `Week` draws one profile per trading week (NQ's opens Sunday 18:00 New York), each from the period's start or, with `Profile side` `Period end`, against its end; `Length` is how many periods are drawn, counted back from the newest one in view (so scrolled back, the periods there draw), or merged in a `Composite` of `Composite of` sessions, days, weeks or months drawn at the right edge, its legend naming them (`VP composite · 13 weeks`). `Length` has no cap: a composite reaches back as far as prints are held, and a 13-week rolling composite is one call. `Prior period POC and VA` carries the previous period's POC, VAH and VAL across the newest, tagged `pPOC`, `pVAH`, `pVAL`; with `Week`, naked POCs are weekly. `chart.get_state`'s `viewport.profiles` lists each profile drawn with its span and POC once it is on screen, and only those: a profile off its bars is not listed.
- `of-draw-report` `chart.get_state`'s `viewport.profiles` lists only the volume profiles with some of their rows or POC, VAH or VAL lines on screen, on time, tick, volume and daily bars: a period with no volume read yet, or priced or spanning off screen, is left out, and so is a fixed-range profile scrolled away; regular-hours periods on daily bars are drawn inside their day's bar and listed. `chart.list_layer_kinds {}` gives each kind with its first sentence and no schema; `{kind}` (an id or words of a title) gives the match in full, `full: true` every kind in full, `limit` caps and `omitted` counts.
- `of-any-timeframe` the footprint and the volume profile draw on every timeframe, 1D included, with nothing in their legend rows. The app's footprint asks for 1-tick rows and the runtime coarsens them on round steps (2, 4, 5, 10 … ticks) until 200 bars fit its 250,000-row budget, rather than refusing; an agent's 5,000-row answer still refuses with its limit. The chart picks the row it draws from the pixels a tick takes, on the same steps past 100 ticks, so cells show only once they are readable. A session profile one bar wide fills its bar.
- `of-profile-range` the rail's `Volume profiles` group holds `Fixed range volume profile` (`alt+shift+p`, two clicks) and `Anchored volume profile` (`alt+shift+v`, one click: from that bar to the newest, growing as prints come in). Each click adds a `volume_profile` study with `Period` `Fixed range` or `Anchored` and its `From` and `To` (a range runs to the end of its last bar), not a drawing; the times are then edited in the study's settings, in the chart's time zone. Once placed, and whenever it is clicked, the study has the drawing tools' style bar over it: `Profile settings` (Show Volume, Buy/Sell, Delta or Vol+Δ, Value area, Row size) and Remove; Escape or a click elsewhere puts it away. A range left without times covers the bars in view. A fixed range draws the same profile on every timeframe and on tick and volume bars, and `viewport.profiles` lists it as `Range` with the same POC on each.
- `of-bubbles` a bubble never moves sideways or with zoom: its x is the centre of its own candle body (with the footprint on, the centre of that bar's stem) and its y is the trade's price, the same point on every frame, scroll and zoom. Every admitted trade keeps its own bubble; nothing is merged or summed. A bubble is a disc in the chart look's `Big buys` or `Big sells` colour at that colour's own opacity up to 75% (the default is up or down at 25%; an opaque colour draws a 75% disc), with a 1 px ring in the full hue, 2 to 10 px in radius as bars widen and up to 2.5 times that by the square root of its size over the filter's minimum (`At least`, or the cut of Auto's tier). Discs paint smallest first so the bigger one sits on top and its ring reads. With `Show size` on and bars at least 5 px wide, every bubble shows its own size, whatever the Auto tier or Fixed size: the figure is set at up to 10.5 px and shrinks, or abbreviates (`1.2k`, `12.5k`, `1.2M`), until it and its halo fit inside that disc; under 5 px bars no bubble carries a figure. The disc is never stretched to fit a figure, and the figures paint largest last over a halo of their own bubble so the biggest trades read on top. Under a footprint a bubble is capped to its bar plus a slight spill and draws over the footprint's cells, so it stays visible on its candle while the cells and figures still read around it. `Plot at` puts it at the event's average price rounded to a tick (the default), first print or last print (`of-bt-zones`).
- `of-bt-zones` with `Zones` `On` each big trade, or only the largest of its bar, projects a band one tick either side of its price for `Bars forward` bars (default 10), in its side's colour at 18% fill, ending where a later bar trades through it. `Plot at` moves marker and band to the event's average price (default), first print or last print, and the legend names a price other than the average (`Big trades first print`).
- `of-fixed` zooming in time or scrolling never merges, sums or moves an event: every admitted trade in view sits at its candle centre and its price, and every one is drawn and under the pointer for its own tooltip, both sides of a fight included. A trade keeps its radius whatever else is in view; bubbles grow a little as bars widen and keep their pixels on a taller price scale.
- `of-grouping` `Join an order's fills` and `Stop 2 ticks past the sweep`, both on by default, decide how prints group into events, live and in history alike (`market.get_big_trades` `joinOrders`, `reachLimit`); with both off prints join by the 5 ms chain alone, so a sweep through many levels is one bubble. They are the only big-trades settings that read the sessions again: charts with the same two share one feed.
- `of-bt-outcome` each big trade knows what happened within 60 s of its last print, read back (`market.get_big_trades` `outcome`) and decided live from the tape alike: pushed (a print `reachTicks` past its far price, its way), absorbed (a print `reachTicks` back past its average, against it) or neither. The bubble looks the same either way; its tooltip card ends on `After 60 s` with `Absorbed · back N ticks`, `Pushed through · N ticks` or `Neither`, and the Analyst's ask adds `Absorbed: price came back N ticks within S`, `Pushed through: N ticks past it within S` or `Neither within 60 s`.
- `of-bt-auto` `Min size` `Auto` (the default) shows the events Tessera's model tiers for their trade date, at or above the cut of the tier `Auto shows` names (`rule.cuts`, sized from the five sessions before): `Low` about the 120th largest event of a session, `Medium` the 45th, `High` the 15th, each pick also showing the tiers above it. Under the row the form reads the day's cuts (`Low ≥ 60 · Medium ≥ 120 · High ≥ 180`) and a hint saying how Auto is sized. The legend reads `Auto <tier> ≥ <cut>`, or `Auto <tier>` before the first answer. With under an hour of events in those sessions the legend reads just `Auto <tier>` and Auto shows no bubble.
- `of-tip` a bubble's tooltip is a card per event under the pointer (three at most, then `N more here`), beside the bubble and flipped left at the chart's right edge: a side dot, `Big trade`, `Buy · lifted the offer` or `Sell · hit the bid`, then Size (`55 contracts`), Price (range and levels), Time (seconds, display time zone), Filled (fills, duration, orders), Tier (`Low`, `Medium` or `High` by the day's cuts, none under the Low cut), and the outcome once decided.
- `of-history` on open the big-trades layer reads back ten trading sessions, a day per read, without a scroll.
- `of-filter` `Min size` is `Auto` (`of-bt-auto`) or `Fixed`. `Fixed` shows `At least` (default 60, never under 1) and `At most` (an empty field reading `None`: no limit), and every event of at least that many lots up to the max. The legend reads `≥ 60`, or `60–200` with a max. Clearing `At most` sets it back to no limit. Switching `Auto` and `Fixed` keeps each mode's own values.
- `of-bt-no-reload` changing the size never reloads: raising `At least`, switching between Auto and Fixed, picking another Auto tier, changing `At most` or toggling `Show size` redraws the bubbles at once with no placeholder in the legend. Lowering `At least` under what the layer holds reads only the missing sizes (`market.get_big_trades` with `maxSize` one under the old floor, over the spans already read) while the bubbles on screen stay, the legend row showing its still placeholder (`of-loading`) only while it reads. A value typed on the way to another (6 on the way to 60) reads nothing.
- `of-cvd-pane` a new CVD pane takes 22% of the plot's height and its visible bars fill it under its legend, with zero in range only when the view's lowest value is near it.
- `of-delta-extras` the `Delta and CVD` study's settings add three things, all off by default and with nothing in the legend row: `CVD style` `Histogram` draws the running total as bars up and down from zero in the `Histogram` plot's colours, with zero always in range; `Highlight delta %` (`Off` by default, steps of 5) keeps every bar whose |delta| is at least that share of its volume at full strength and fades the rest to 30% (bar delta, CVD candles and the histogram), and on a CVD line dots those bars in the delta colours; `Divergences` marks each bar whose price closed against its delta (up on negative delta, down on positive) with a 7 px triangle pointing the way price closed, in the up or down colour, just past the bar's mark on its delta's side. Triangles and dots draw only where bars are wide enough to keep them apart.
- `of-zone` an imbalance zone price has not traded back through ends at the last bar.
- `of-stacked-imbalance` `Stacked imbalance` is its own study (Add indicator › Order flow, ⌘K `Stacked imbalance: show or hide`), off until added, with `Imbalance ratio`, `Minimum stack`, `Min volume`, `Min difference`, `Include zero`, `Row size` (Auto: the contract's footprint rows, NQ 4 ticks) and `Tested zones`. Its legend reads `Stacks 3:1 ×3 4 ticks`. With the footprint off, at any zoom, each stack draws as a zone from its bar to the right in the ask (buy) or bid (sell) colour, ending at the bar that trades back into it; `Tested zones` off hides those ended zones. The footprint's own zones are unchanged. `scripts/stacked-imbalance.ts [out] [ui dir] [tag]` adds it from the picker on a busy 1m NQ chart, opens its settings, and makes and fires its alert (`al-stack`).
- `of-loading` nothing is painted over or behind the bars while a study reads: no band, no grey, no `reading the session`, `reading older bars` or seconds counter. Until a study's first answer its legend row shows a still placeholder (the kit's `Skeleton`, no words, the shared slow shimmer, still under reduced motion) where its values go, and the bars stay plain candles. While the runtime is still fetching sessions of the study's days to load, the legend row says how far it is after its values, muted: `Loading · 1 of 2 sessions` (the footprint, the CVD, the volume profile and big trades alike); the words go once every session is held. With no room, a flow row drops its extras first (the `3:1` ratio and the row size), then says `Loading 0/1`, then `Loading…` with the name cut to an ellipsis and the `≈` mark dropped; it is never cut at the chart's edge (`chart/settings/legendFit.ts`; the stills at a chart's 100 px minimum width are on `screenshots/568`). Every answer is data: the footprint, the volume profile and the big trades draw what the runtime answers at once. An answer that says `filling` (the runtime is asking the connection's tick replay for a gap) is asked again on a backoff until it settles. The newest footprints draw each such answer as it comes, newest bars first, so the first bars show within seconds of a cold start; older footprint pages, a session's profile and a day's bubbles take only the settled answer, in one change, so they never grow row by row; a session profile with gaps waits while its answer is filling, then draws partial. `../scripts/orderflow-loading.ts <dir> [ui dir] [pr|main]` starts its own sim on the fake Rithmic login with each history request held 400 ms (`TESSERA_TEST_MARKET=rithmic`, `TESSERA_TEST_HISTORY_MS=400`), adds a footprint to NQ 1m from a cold store, samples the legend every 200 ms (`cold-loading.png`, `cold-settled.png`, `cold-legend.json`), times the first footprint bars, then opens the desk again and counts the history requests the second open made (`market.saved_trades` `pulled`, none on the PR build). A bar in a gap (session time with no stored or recorded prints) is a plain candle, and no legend says anything about it: no `no tick history`, no `since …`. A failed read shows `stopped: …`. `../scripts/held-reads.ts <dir> 1m|1h` holds some of the runtime's answers so the placeholder can be seen beside what has loaded, then releases them.
- `of-tick-gaps` the app fills a gap in the prints by itself. A gap the connection's tick replay can fill is replayed while its answer says `filling`, and nothing marks it meanwhile; once filled, the bars there are footprints like the rest. A gap a settled answer still has (the replay refused it, or keeps nothing that old) is marked once, faintly, by a thin muted strip along the top of the time axis under exactly the bars it touches, the same strip for the footprint, the volume profile, big trades and the CVD, drawn once however many of them miss it. Hovering the strip on the time axis shows `No trade history 10:00–10:03` for the span under the pointer. No legend row says `no tick history`; when the login refused sessions of a study's days to load, its legend row says `Older history isn’t available on this login` after its values. The hub remembers a refused span in the tick store: neither it nor anything older is asked again before the next session opens, even after a restart (`crates/tessera-runtime/tests/orderflow_loading.rs`). `../scripts/tick-gaps.ts <dir> [ui dir] [pr|main]` starts its own sim twice on a Saturday clock with Friday's session stored around a three-minute hole at 10:00 New York and adds the footprint, volume profile and big trades to NQ Z6 1m over 09:40 to 10:25: once with replays allowed (`replayed-filling.png` while the settled answers are held back, `replayed-settled.png` filled with no mark), once with replays refused (`refused-settled.png` and `refused-axis.png`, the strip under 10:00 to 10:02 and no words). It saves each scene's `market.get_footprint` answer. To shoot main the same way pass main's runtime as `TESSERA_TEST_RUNTIME_BIN` and main's `dist` as the ui dir.
- `of-stored-history` with the market closed and nothing live, every order-flow study builds from the ticks stored on the machine for the range in view: the footprint, the CVD, the volume profile and big trades fill every stored session at once, however many holes lie between them. A day not stored, or a hole in one, is plain candles under the footprint with the time axis's faint gap strip under it and no words (`of-tick-gaps`); the CVD has no value there and carries its session's total past a hole. `../scripts/stored-history.ts <dir> [ui dir]` starts its own sim on a Saturday clock (`TESSERA_TEST_NOW`) with the Tuesday to Friday sessions before it stored and Thursday holding an hour's hole (`TESSERA_TEST_STORED`), Monday not stored and replays refused (`TESSERA_TEST_REPLAY=refuse`), and shoots NQ Z6 15m over the week: candles, the footprint while its answers are held, the footprint and the CVD settled, and Monday alone. It saves `market.get_footprint`'s and `market.get_delta`'s answers for the week beside the stills. To shoot main the same way, build main's `tessera-test-runtime` with the sim knobs commit cherry-picked, and pass its path as `TESSERA_TEST_RUNTIME_BIN` and main's `dist` as the ui dir.
- `of-type-menu` the chart toolbar's `Chart type` menu (`button "Chart type: Candles"`) lists Candles, Bars and Line, then `Footprint` and `Volume profile` as checkboxes: checked when the chart draws that layer, a click turns it on or off (the same toggle as `alt+shift+f` and `alt+p`). One is greyed out, with what it needs (`Needs trades with aggressor`), only when the runtime's `chart.list_layer_kinds` says the chart's connection cannot draw it.
- `of-imbalance` a figure against an empty row on the other side of the diagonal ("7 against 0") is not coloured as an imbalance and starts no stack or zone, unless the footprint's `Include zero` is on. Imbalances and zones are found on the contract's rows (NQ 4 ticks) or the footprint's fixed `Row size`, so zooming never adds or removes one.
- `of-type-everywhere` the chart's two other `Chart type` menus list the same: the right-click's `Chart type` submenu is the toolbar's own list (greyed out the same way), and the header More menu's (`button "Chart: more"`) `Chart type` submenu lists Candles, Bars, Line, then `Footprint` and `Volume profile` with their keys, ticked while the chart draws them; a pick there toggles the layer as the keys do.
- `of-dom-orders` each of your orders on the DOM is a chip whose title says what it is in words (`Your buy limit 1 · 12 ahead of it`, `Your stop · 1 · placed once the entry fills`). Your orders above or below the ladder are marked at its top or bottom edge, nearest first (`▲ TP 20,660.50`, `▼ SL 20,630.50`) beside the last price's mark; a click on one shows that order and holds the view there.
- `of-dom-header` the DOM's header reads `Orders`, `Bid`, `Price`, `Ask`, `Orders`: orders at the outer edges, contracts beside the price.
- `of-dom-docks` a DOM or time & sales opened on the Day desk docks right of the chart showing its contract (else the active chart), and the charts of that row give up its width from the widest down, so neither chart is left narrow.
- `of-days-to-load` every order-flow study (footprint, `Delta and CVD`, volume profile, big trades) has `Days to load` in its settings, default 2: the current session and the one before. The runtime asks the connection only for those sessions' missing prints (`daysToLoad` on `market.get_footprint`, `market.get_volume_profile`, `market.get_big_trades` and the CVD's `indicators.series` inputs); older bars draw from prints already saved, and big trades walk back that many sessions. Each answer's `loaded` says how many of those sessions it holds (`sessions`, `held`, `refused`). The chart's own bar and print reads go ahead of the saved-data downloader, which waits until they end. Seconds charts are built from stored one-second bars, so a second read asks nothing. Settings › Data lists `History pulled` per login: requests and bytes today and over seven days.
- `of-scrollback` scrolled back as far as the chart holds bars, the footprint and the session profiles fill every older session the view shows (`of-loading`): the footprints hold as many bars as the chart (50,000), each older footprint window is read once, paging back from the answer's `firstOpen` until it is null, and session profiles are read by the view rather than as the last ten. The CVD is the runtime's `indicators.series` for `delta`: the runtime reads the order flow of the bars a reach adds before it answers, so each older stretch arrives whole in one span update, without any `market.get_footprint` of its own.
- `of-profile-options` the Volume profile's settings popover offers four profile types under `Show` (Volume, Bid/Ask split, Delta, Volume and delta: the delta in a column beside the volume), `Length`, `Hours` (Whole session, RTH only, RTH and ETH apart), `Extend POC and VA lines` and `Developing VA`. Each is saved in the layer's params. The legend names a type or hours other than the default in words (`VP RTH delta`, `VP week`), and with RTH only the POC, VAH and VAL tags are the RTH profile's.
- `of-profile-delta` with `Show` `Delta` or `Volume and delta`, every period (`Session`, `Day`, `Week`, `Month`, `Composite`, and ranges) draws each price row's delta (buy less sell volume) in the buy or sell colour, alone or in a narrow column beside the volume. The legend adds the newest profile's total, `Δ +1,234` in the delta colours, after its POC; hovering a profile for the Analyst names its delta beside its volume; `viewport.profiles` gives every drawn profile's `delta`. That figure is the delta study's (`Delta and CVD`) bar deltas summed over the profile's span: prints with no side count in neither. Volume, the default, adds nothing to the legend.
- `of-dom-width` a DOM in the desk's grid is never narrower than its Bid, Price and Ask need (207 px): as the window narrows the charts give up the width, and a DOM too narrow for its order counts drops the two `Orders` columns first, its header reading `Bid`, `Price`, `Ask`.
- `of-tape-filter` the time & sales' `Min size` (its settings popover, from the tape's right-click `Settings…`) is a box you type any size into, `Every size` (0, the default) showing every print, a fraction of a coin included; its − and + step 0, 1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 250 and on. Only prints at least that size show, live and in the history read under them, and the tab menu's `Settings…` names it (`≥ 20`).
- `of-tape-speed` the time & sales' `Speed` is `Slow` (3 rows a second), `Medium` (8), `Fast` (20) or `Live` (the default, every print as it comes). A paced tape shows a lone print at once and spreads a burst out evenly, never more than 2 s behind: a burst it cannot show at its speed within 2 s comes faster, evenly. Pausing under the pointer counts the waiting rows in `N new`; leaving, `Home` or the pill shows the newest at once. Both settings are saved in the tile's params (`layout.get`, `minSize` and `speed`) and survive a reload.
- `of-market-profile` `Market profile (TPO)` is its own study (Add indicator › Order flow, search `tpo` or `market profile`, ⌘K `Market profile: show or hide`), off until added. Its defaults: `Period` `Session` (or `Day`, `Week`, `Month`), `Length` 5, `TPO size` `30 min` (or 5, 10, 15, 60), `Draw as` `Letters`, `Hours` `Chart`, `Value area %` 70, `Row size` `Auto`, `Initial balance` on; its legend reads `TPO 30m POC 20,914.75` (`TPO 15m blocks`, `TPO week 30m`). Each period draws from its first bar in view: one letter per bracket at every row its bracket traded, `A` to `Z` then `a` to `z` from each session's open (starting again at the next session in a week or month), stacked left to right in time order; out-of-value letters muted, value-area letters brighter, the POC row brightest, with the POC, VAH and VAL lines and a 2 px initial-balance bar (the first hour from the regular open) at the left. `Blocks` draws the same cells as squares in the profile colours, and letters fall back to blocks wherever they would be too small to read (a week on 1h, a narrow tile). It is the same engine as the volume profile: `market.get_volume_profile` with `tpoMinutes` answers each profile's `tpo` (brackets `[start, letter, low, high]`, TPO POC, VAH, VAL, `ibFrom`, `ibHigh`, `ibLow`), and `viewport.profiles` lists each drawn TPO profile with those POC, VAH and VAL. `../scripts/market-profile.ts [out] [ui dir] [tag]` adds it from the library on a fresh sim, checks the stored defaults, switches it to blocks at 15 minutes in its settings and to weeks, and holds every drawn profile's POC, VAH and VAL to the runtime's (`<tag>-report.json`, no failures); with main's `dist` it shoots the library finding nothing.
- `of-effort` Effort absorption (Special group) arms a zone over a streak of qualifying bars on either side: buy bars (close ≥ open, delta ≥ 0) draw in `buyColor`, sell bars (close < open, delta < 0) in `sellColor`. A bar of one side ends the other side's streak; each zone projects `extendBars` bars and ends where price trades back into it.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- `button "Tessera menu: <workspace>"`, `menuitem "New from preset"`, `menuitem "Scalp 1m order flow, Trade, DOM and time & sales"`. `main "Workspace"` then holds `region "NQ Z6 1m"`, `region "NQ Z6 · DOM"` and `region "NQ Z6 · T&S"`.
- The footprint shows figures once bars are wide: scroll the wheel up over the chart, about 15 notches from the preset's view, until bars are near 90 px.

## Driving it

- **Figure sizes (`of-figure-sizes`).** `../scripts/footprint-figures.ts <dir> [ui dir] [pr|main] [bars …]` starts its own sim runtime, sets the default workspace's `chart-1` to ES 1m with a footprint, maximises it, and shoots it at `chart.set_viewport` `lastBars` 8, 11, 13, 16 and 24 (or the counts given) into `<name>-<n>-bars.png` at 1600 by 1000. On the frozen clock the figures show through 16 bars, smaller from 14, and the cells take over from 17; main's build drops them from 14. With `TESSERA_TEST_RUNTIME_BIN` and another build's `dist` it shoots that build the same way.
- **Footprint modes (`of-footprint-modes`).** `../scripts/footprint-modes.ts <dir> [ui dir] [pr|main]` starts its own sim runtime, sets the default workspace's `chart-1` to ES 1m with a footprint, maximises it at 12 bars and shoots `Bid × Ask`, `Trades`, `Bid × Ask histogram`, `Dim below %` 40, `Max level` `Volume` and `Trades` with `Max level` `Trades` into `<name>-<scene>.png`; then the histogram with `Dim below %` 30 and `Max level` `Volume` on 1m, 5m, 1h and 1D (`<name>-all-on-<tf>.png`). It fails when a call is refused, a legend row has words, or a row of `market.get_footprint` with volume counts no print. It also shoots the footprint's settings (`<name>-settings.png`). With `main` the runtime refuses the new inputs, so every scene is main's default footprint and the run exits non-zero.
- **Volume profiles by period and range (`of-profile-periods`, `of-profile-range`, `of-any-timeframe`).** `../scripts/volume-profiles.ts <dir> [ui dir] [pr|main]` starts its own sim runtime and, on the default workspace's NQ chart, adds and shoots each profile type in turn: on 1h a weekly profile, a fixed range (Tue 8 Sep 09:30 to Fri 2 Oct 14:00), an anchored profile and a 13-week composite; on 1D RTH session profiles, weeks, the range and the composite; on 1000-contract volume bars and 500-tick bars session, RTH session, weekly, anchored and composite profiles. Then it places a fixed range with `alt+shift+p` and two clicks, opens its settings and types a new `From`. Each scene's `viewport.profiles` and every answer go in `<name>-report.json`; it exits non-zero when a scene draws nothing or the drawn report lacks it. With `main`, range and anchored profiles are main's drawings. Pass main's `dist` and `TESSERA_TEST_RUNTIME_BIN` with main's `tessera-test-runtime` to shoot main the same way.
- **Every timeframe (`of-any-timeframe`).** `../scripts/daily-orderflow.ts <dir> [ui dir] [pr|main]` starts its own sim runtime, sets the default workspace's NQ chart to 1D, adds the footprint and the volume profile, and shoots the default view and a close-up of 21 Sep to 2 Oct, saving each scene's legend rows and `viewport.profiles` and `market.get_footprint` 1D over 200 bars at `rowTicks` 1 in `<name>-report.json`. It exits non-zero if a legend row has words, the profile drew nothing or the read was refused. The sim holds prints for its newest 20 sessions, so the footprint draws on those bars. ## Gotchas

- `tessera-test-runtime` exits when its stdin closes. Started by hand, keep stdin open (`tail -f /dev/null | …`).
- Big trades on Auto appear only once the model's five sessions hold an hour of events and the busy tape prints events at least the Low cut (`market.get_big_trades` lists the cuts in `rule.cuts`, null while still sizing); until then the legend reads just `Auto <tier>` (`Auto low`) and no bubble shows, so a short run can show none. A size shows its events either way.
- Stop a runtime you started by its process id, never by name, and delete its data directory and info file.
