# Desktop updates

A check for a new build only looks. A build it finds shows a quiet card in the bottom-right corner, above the status bar, with Later and Update. Update opens a confirmation that says Tessera will restart, and counts the working orders, open positions and analyst sessions in progress. Only its Restart and update downloads, installs and relaunches. Once the download starts the window can be hidden; the download keeps going under the rail's item and waits as ready if it finishes out of sight.

## Sub-features

- `prompt` is the card: `region "Update"` with `Tessera <version> is available`, the notes cut to two lines, `button "Show all"` when they were cut, `button "Later"` and `button "Update"`. No dialog is open while it shows.
- `snooze` is Later: the card hides for a day. Timer checks still run while it is snoozed, and keep it hidden while the same build is newest; a newer build replaces the snooze with its card. A person's check shows it again at once; the next launch offers it again.
- `rediscovery` is the timer and window focus: checks run a minute after launch, every thirty minutes, and when the main window regains focus. A standing card naming an older build becomes a card naming the newest one; an up-to-date check leaves `Up to date · checked HH:MM` and the next check comes within thirty minutes.
- `confirm` is `dialog "Update to Tessera <version>"`: `Tessera will restart to install it.`, `button "Not now"` and `button "Restart and update"`. With nothing open, focus is on Restart and update.
- `warnings` are the dialog's counts: `You have 2 working orders and 1 open position. They stay at your broker, but Tessera will be offline for a moment while it restarts.` and `1 analyst session is in progress. …`. Focus is on Not now. Counts come from the orders, positions and agent-session stores, so they match `portfolio.get_orders`, `portfolio.get_positions` and `agent.list_sessions`.
- `download` is Restart and update: `Downloading <version> · 42%` with a thin bar and `button "Hide"`, then, if the window is still showing when it finishes, `Installing <version>. Tessera restarts in a moment.` with every button off. A failure says `The update couldn’t be downloaded.` (or installed) with `button "Close"` and `button "Try again"`; Try again checks the build is still offered before it downloads again.
- `background` is a hidden download. Hide, Escape or a click outside the window hides it and never cancels it. The rail item reads `Updating` with a thin bar under the glyph (`button "Downloading <version> · 51%"`); the Tessera menu and Settings › App read `Show update`, and Settings' help reads `Downloading <version> · 51%`. Any way in (the rail, the menu, Settings, the macOS menu bar) reopens the same download at the same percent, with no check. A download that finishes hidden never restarts Tessera: the item reads `Restart` (`button "Update ready · Restart to install <version>"`), the menu and Settings read `Restart to update…`, and opening it shows the confirmation again with today's warnings, `button "Not now"` (hides it) and `button "Restart and update"`. A hidden failure turns the item into `Try again` (`button "Couldn’t update to <version>. Try again"`) and opening it shows the failure with Try again. The build being downloaded or ready is kept in the window's storage (`tessera.updateDownload`), so a reload or a relaunch checks it is still offered and downloads it again, hidden, ending ready; a build no longer offered is let go (a newer one shows its card).
- `check` is the four ways to check at once: the rail's item above Feedback, the Tessera menu's `menuitem "Check for updates"`, Settings › App's `Tessera desktop` row (`button "Check for updates"`, help `Up to date · checked 14:02` after a check that found nothing) and, on macOS, Tessera › Check for Updates… in the menu bar. Beyond the rail item's own words, a check that finds nothing says nothing else.
- `rail` is that item, `button "Check for updates"` shown as `Updates` with the update glyph, sized like Feedback and Settings. A person's check from anywhere turns it into `Checking…` (`button "Checking for updates…"`), then `Up to date` (`button "Tessera is up to date"`, a check glyph) for four seconds when nothing is newer, with no card and no dialog. A failed check reads `Try again` (`button "Couldn’t check for updates. Try again"`) beside the card's `Couldn’t check for updates.`, and a click checks again. A found build opens the card, and its Update still opens the confirmation. The timer's and focus's own checks leave the item at rest.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Only the desktop app has updates. In a browser, and so on the e2e session, `appHost().updates` is null: no card, no rail item, no menu item, no Settings button.

## Driving it

`../scripts/update-prompt.ts [evidence dir] [ui dir] [pr|main]` starts its own sim runtime and opens the window as the desktop app would, with the Tauri shell's commands stood in for inside the page (`update_check` offers a build when the script says so, `update_download` sends progress for about 6 s, `update_install` never returns, as a relaunch would). It first drives a background download in a window of its own, with the stand-in download slowed to about 18 s: `pr-bg-dialog-downloading.png`, a click outside the window, then the rail mid-download (`pr-bg-rail-downloading.png`, `pr-bg-rail-downloading-rail.png`, `pr-bg-window-downloading.png`), checking the bar under the icon keeps moving, then the rail item reopens the same download (`pr-bg-reopened.png`, the percent no lower than the bar's), Escape hides it again, the hidden download ends ready (`pr-bg-rail-ready.png`) with only `update_check,update_download` heard, a reload picks it up again (`pr-bg-relaunch-downloading.png`) and ends ready without a window, and the ready build's window (`pr-bg-ready-window.png`) installs only on Restart and update. Then, in a new window, it checks through the macOS menu's event, then shoots `pr-prompt.png` and its card (`pr-prompt-card.png`), swaps the fake endpoint to a newer build, regains focus and shoots the replaced card (`pr-prompt-newer.png`, `pr-prompt-newer-card.png`). It then shoots `pr-confirm-clear.png`, `pr-confirm-warnings.png` (after a Paper market buy, a buy limit, a sell limit and a stand-in Claude turn with three queued messages), `pr-downloading.png` and `pr-installing.png`, and checks the dialog's words and focus against the runtime's own orders, positions and sessions (saved beside the stills). It asserts the shell heard only `update_check` twice, once from the menu and once from focus, until Restart and update, then `update_download` and `update_install`. Before that flow, in a window of its own, it drives the rail item: `pr-rail-rest.png` (the whole rail) and `pr-rail-rest-foot.png`, then with the stand-in check taking 2 s `pr-rail-checking.png` and `pr-rail-up-to-date.png`, then a failing check's `pr-rail-failed.png` and `pr-rail-failed-window.png`, then Try again with a build on offer: `pr-rail-prompt.png`, `pr-rail-prompt-rail.png`, `pr-rail-prompt-card.png` and the confirmation its Update opens, `pr-rail-confirm.png`. It checks the item sits right above Feedback at Feedback's size, reads each state's words, opens no dialog for Up to date, and that the shell heard only `update_check`. `main` with main's `dist` shoots `main-rail-rest.png`, `main-rail-rest-foot.png` and `main-window.png`, then starts a download, clicks outside the window and shoots `main-bg-dialog-downloading.png`, `main-bg-rail-downloading.png`, `main-bg-rail-downloading-rail.png` and `main-bg-window-downloading.png` for the comparison. It needs no e2e session.

## Gotchas

- The stand-in shell proves the window's flow, not the Rust commands: `cargo test --manifest-path apps/desktop/src-tauri/Cargo.toml updates` pins their event shapes, and a real update is step 4 of `docs/release-smoke-test.md`.
- `orders.submit` from a script is a draft until `approvals.resolve` approves it, as in the e2e suite.
