# Connections

The user's connections and their logins and keys. Settings › Connections is their one home: one row per login, levels provider, drafted levels source and paper trading, each with its state, what it gives and whom it serves, and the actions its kind and state call for. The status bar at the window's foot shows every connection but paper trading and drafts with its state, and opens its row. Settings › Levels keeps only how levels show.

## Sub-features

- `cn-status` the status bar's `navigation "Connection status"` holds one `button "<name>: <state>"` per connection (a dot, its short name, its latency while it serves); clicking one opens Settings › Connections on its row. With no connection it holds `button "Connect Rithmic"`.
- `cn-attribution` while a Rithmic login is connected or late, the status bar ends with `button "Trading Platform by Rithmic Powered by OMNE"`: Rithmic's official grey marks (`apps/desktop/src/assets/rithmic/`, from Rithmic's artwork zip, never redrawn), with the tooltip `Rithmic and OMNE notices`. It opens Settings › App on `region "Notices"`, which lists Rithmic's four copyright and trademark lines at all times. A pop-out window's footer shows the same marks as `group "Rithmic attribution"`. Connecting, down, off or no Rithmic login: no marks. Rithmic Commercial Conformance asks for both (#300).
- `cn-rows` `region "Your connections"` holds one `group "<name>"` per connection: a dot and the name, a line of what it gives (`Prices, depth by order, accounts and fills`, `Levels for NQ, ES`) and whom it serves, its state (`12 ms`, `Connected`, `Off`, the runtime's words for a problem), then its actions.
- `cn-actions` a Rithmic login offers `Test`, `Edit` and `Disconnect` (`Connect` once off), plus `Reconnect` when it is down, `Try again` when the keychain did not give its saved login (`credential: "unreadable"`) and `Log in again` when its login is missing. A levels provider offers `Test`, `Edit` and `Remove`; a draft `Review…` and `Discard`; paper trading `Commission`.
- `cn-edit` a login's `Edit` opens `group "Edit <name>"` under its row: the login form with its firm fixed, its user and gateway filled, `Replace`, and `Remove` (logs out and deletes the saved login). A levels provider's shows `Asks` (its schedule and today's requests), `History` (`Backfill…`, when its spec declares the vendor's history API) and a link to how its levels show; `Edit spec…` opens its sheet, where a new key replaces the saved one and an empty key keeps it. A source an earlier Tessera had built in shows down with the words `built into an earlier Tessera … Add it again with Any API, with the analyst…`; a spec saved with its id keeps its saved key.
- `cn-add` `button "Add connection"` lists `Rithmic…`, then under Levels `Any API, with the analyst…` and `Write a spec…`, and nothing else: no provider is built in, and the journal's file import lives in the Journal. Rithmic opens a short form above the list (`region "Add a Rithmic login"`) with `Test`, `Test and save` and `Cancel`; `Any API, with the analyst…` asks the analyst to draft a spec, and `Write a spec…` opens the spec sheet. Every secret field's help starts `Saved securely on this Mac` (`this computer` elsewhere).
- `cn-exchanges` `region "Market data by exchange"` and `region "Orders by exchange"` pick, for every exchange and for each one, the connection its data comes from and the account its orders go from.
- `cn-onboarding` a first run with no market-data connection opens on `dialog "Connect your Rithmic login"` with the same login form, its help lines (`The nearest to you is quickest`, `Saved securely on this computer, sent only to Rithmic`) and `Not now`, `Test` and `Connect`.
- `cn-phase` a login's state is its connection phase as the runtime sends it (`connection`, and `accounts` when its account plants stand elsewhere), worded on the desk alone (`connectionWords` in `apps/desktop/src/shell/deskWords.ts`): `Connection lost. Reconnecting, 2nd try.`, `Logged out. Logging in again.`, `Session ended. Reconnect in Connections.`, `Login refused. Check the user name and password.`, `Off`. A refused history login leaves the prices live: the login reads `Rithmic refused history for this login. Prices stay live; charts show only what is saved.` and market orders still go.
- `cn-taken-over` a Rithmic login logged out by another session of the same login (a ForcedLogout) logs in once more by itself; logged out again within ten minutes, it stays down and its row and status read `Another session of this login took over. Close it, then reconnect in Connections.`, with `Reconnect`. Order flow drawn across the logout has no gap strip on the time axis once the hub has replayed the gap, and no legend words while it does (`order-flow.md`, `of-tick-gaps`).
- `cn-chart-connect` a chart no connection serves says `No connection serves <symbol> now` with `Connect Rithmic` (Settings › Connections with the Rithmic form open) while no connection serves prices, else `Connections`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Settings in the rail (Ctrl+,): Connections is its first page.
- Any button of the status bar's `navigation "Connection status"`.

## Driving it with the e2e MCP

Preconditions: the baseline in `README.md`. The sim's Rithmic is a fake gateway and its levels vendor a fake API (`Acme`, key `demo`), both inside the sim runtime, with an in-memory keychain: nothing leaves the machine and nothing is kept after the session.

- **Status bar.** `navigation "Connection status"` holds `button "Mock market: 0 ms"` and `button "Mock gamma levels: Connected"`.
- **Rows.** Open Settings. `region "Your connections"` holds `group "Mock market"`, `group "Mock gamma levels"` and `group "Paper trading"`. Mock market offers no action (it is no login); Mock gamma levels offers `Test`, `Edit` and `Remove`.
- **Rithmic.** `Add connection` › `Rithmic…`. In `region "Add a Rithmic login"` pick the firm `Tessera Test` (its gateways are `Chicago Area` and `Europe`), any user, and the password `demo`; any other password is refused in plain words, with no Rithmic code: `Rithmic refused the login. Check the user name and password. Nothing saved.`. `Test and save` adds `group "Tessera Test · …"`, healthy with a latency, serving one read-only account named after the user, and the status bar shows it. `Test`, `Edit` › `Replace`, `Disconnect` (state `Off`, the status bar's dot off), `Connect`, and `Edit` › `Remove` each act on the runtime: read `runtime.ts <port> connections.list` and `connections.list_credentials`.
- **A levels source from a spec.** `Add connection` › `Write a spec…`, switch it to JSON and paste the spec of `levels::spec` in `crates/tessera-testkit/src/levels.rs` (base URL `https://api.acme.test`, `Authorization: Bearer`, NQ from the ticker `NDX` with a conversion request). With the key `demo` its `Test` reads `Zero Gamma` and the other levels; any other key is refused (401). Save it, then `Test`, `Edit spec…` (an empty key keeps the saved one), and `Remove` with its confirm. `TESSERA_TEST_LEVELS=live` (docs/dev.md §9) lets a source reach a real vendor instead, for a proof through the analyst with the user's own key.
- **Onboarding and the chart's Connect.** The e2e session's sim always has its mock market, so neither shows on its own there. A sim started by hand with `TESSERA_TEST_MARKET=rithmic` opens on the onboarding by itself (`first-week.md`). Drive one page with Playwright from a script outside the repository: `page.routeWebSocket(/\/ws/, …)` relays every frame and drops each object with `kind: "market_data"` from the server's frames (the page then reads as a fresh install), and answers each `tools.invoke` of `market.get_candles` for `NQZ6.CME` with the runtime's refusal for an unserved contract (`{ok: false, status: "error", error: {code: "not_found", message: "unknown instrument NQZ6.CME", …}}`). Open the launch link: the onboarding dialog shows. Tap `Not now`: `region "NQ Z6 5m"` reads `No connection serves NQ Z6 now` and `button "Connect Rithmic"`, which opens Settings › Connections with `region "Add a Rithmic login"`.

- **Reconnecting.** `node .agents/skills/verify-tessera/scripts/rithmic-reconnecting.ts [evidence dir] [ui dir] [pr|main]` starts its own sim runtime with `TESSERA_TEST_RITHMIC=dropped`, adds the `Tessera Test` login with the password `demo`, and reads its row and its status-bar button: `Connection lost. Reconnecting, 2nd try.` It saves `<scene>-connections.png`, `<scene>-status-bar.png` and `<scene>-words.txt`.
- **Errors in plain words.** A provider's refusal reaches the screen as what happened and what to do, never its code (`apps/desktop/src/shell/deskWords.ts`). `node .agents/skills/verify-tessera/scripts/plain-errors.ts [evidence dir] [ui dir] [pr|main]` starts its own sim runtime twice: once with every `market.get_candles` answered by Rithmic's `request rejected: [13] permission denied`, where `region "NQ Z6 5m"` reads `NQ Z6 did not load. Not available on this login. Ask your broker about access.`, and once for the refused login above. It saves each screen and its words as `<scene>-chart` and `<scene>-login`.

`e2e/agent/connections.e2e.ts` runs the Rithmic flow deterministically, the marks and the Notices included; `crates/tessera-runtime/tests/sim_fakes.rs` adds, tests, keeps and removes the fake vendor's spec.

## Gotchas

- `Try again` and `Log in again` need a saved login the keychain cannot read, which the sim's in-memory keychain never produces. `crates/tessera-runtime/tests/connections.rs` drives them on a fake keychain (`after_an_update_a_saved_login_is_never_taken_for_none`), and `apps/desktop/test/connectors.test.ts` pins each row's action.
- The sim's fake gateway never sends a ForcedLogout, so `cn-taken-over` is proven on the fake Rithmic gateway in `crates/tessera-rithmic/tests/forced_logout.rs` (one more login, the gap replayed until every print is held once and no gap is left; a second logout stays down with no further login) and its words by `apps/desktop/test/desk-words.test.ts` against `fixtures/connections/taken-over.json`.
- The sim's fake login has no plants to drop, so the phase's edges are proven on the fake Rithmic gateway: every transition in `crates/tessera-rithmic/src/link.rs`; a stop during a reconnect stays stopped, a ForcedLogout after a stop logs nothing in and a refused history login keeps the prices live in `tests/sessions.rs`; the accounts stop waiting once another login took over, and stay stopped when stopped while logging in again, in `src/broker/tests.rs`.
- A Rithmic account's own contract limits show in the Accounts tile as `Firm’s contract limits`; `crates/tessera-rithmic/src/broker/tests.rs` and the fake gateway in `crates/tessera-rithmic/tests/orders.rs` prove them.
- The account gate's `unreadable` screen needs Tessera Cloud, which the sim runtime does not have (`tests/account.rs` covers it).
