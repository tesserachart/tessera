# Tessera verification map

This directory is the maintained source for verifying Tessera's user-facing behaviour on the sim runtime. Read this index, then the feature's file.

## Baseline

- A new e2e session, connected, with `runtime.ts <port> doctor` printing `ok` (`../SKILL.md`).
- Three paper accounts: `Paper` (`paper-main`, $100,000, the one the Trade ticket starts on), `Paper 2` and `Paper 3` (`paper-2`, `paper-3`, $50,000 each).
- The Desk area, on the default workspace `Day · NQ/ES`: `region "NQ Z6 5m"` (layout tile `chart-1`) and `region "ES Z6 1m"` (`chart-2`), and the Trade ticket on `Paper, $100,000`, flat.
- The frozen market: NQ Z6 bid 20,641.25, ask 20,641.50; ES Z6 5,583.25.

## Conventions

- Aim by accessible name (`role "name"` in `observe`), never by coordinates.
- Prove each end state on screen and from the runtime with `../scripts/runtime.ts`.
- What one feature changes stays for the rest of the session. Open a new session to start another feature from the baseline.

## Features

- [Desk charts](desk-charts.md): a chart tile's timeframe (any typed interval: 15s, 90m, 2D, 377T) and symbol, stored in the workspace's layout; the view an agent asks for, or why not; hiding one chart's price.
- [Themes](themes.md): one theme for the whole app and its charts: seven built-ins in five families (Tessera, Solarized, Night Owl, Nord, Gruvbox; dark and light), a switch that repaints every surface live, themes the trader saves, duplicates, renames, exports, imports and deletes, every app colour in Settings › Appearance, and the agent's read, set and save.
- [Drawings from other charts](drawing-sources.md): a drawing stays on the chart it was made on; a chart shows another chart's drawings only when its Settings… pick that chart, by chart and not by timeframe, and a new chart picks none.
- [Drawing tools](drawing-tools.md): cross line, trend angle (its degrees), fib extension (three anchors, its target levels) and fib fan on the drawing rail, edited, styled, kept in templates and drawn by the agent.
- [Colour picker](colour-picker.md): the one visual picker every colour choice uses (field, hue, opacity, swatches, recent, hex), live while dragged, one undo step per drag on a drawing.
- [Indicator values](indicators.md): every indicator's values kept in the runtime, the same at a bar when computed, after a reopen, after a timeframe switch back and scrolled back, with the CVD's newest value first; the opening range's levels the same on every interval.
- [Chart studies](chart-studies.md): a new chart starts with candles only; the Standard and Order flow templates and the Scalp story add their studies only when picked, and a study hides or removes from its legend and stays gone.
- [Chart templates](chart-templates.md): the `Template: <name>` chip in each chart's toolbar reads that chart's template (`· modified` once the chart leaves it) and opens the only template menu: save changes, save as new, rename, copy, the list with summaries and the default's star, and Manage templates…; applying one changes that chart alone.
- [Chart levels](chart-levels.md): the default charts start with no levels; Prior high, a mock gamma level or the initial balance turned on by hand draws and stays in the layout.
- [Key overlay](key-overlay.md): tapping or holding ⌥ or ⌘ alone lists every action on that modifier from the command registry, over the focused panel, without taking the focus; a typed key or name highlights a row, Enter or a click runs it, Esc dismisses.
- [Icons](icons.md): one icon per meaning, sharp at 1x (each icon draws the pixel cut for its size) and at 2x (the 1.5 px stroke), checked over every area by `scripts/icon-sharpness.ts`.
- [Desk panels](desk-panels.md): one header on every panel (scope, Split chart in the centre, Pop out, Maximise, More, Close); the Panels library (`+` on every tab row, Add panel on the empty desk, the app menu, ⌘K) brings any closed panel back where it was and focuses an open one, never a copy; maximise any panel; pop out any panel and dock it back; a DOM or T&S on Active chart follows the next chart when its own closes.
- [Workspaces](workspaces.md): saving like a document (the edited mark, Save, Revert to saved, the leave prompt, recovery after a crash), a new workspace's instrument, new from preset, Save as, Reset to default, switching, the switch back that keeps the desk mounted, the calm first load and the workspace's windows.
- [Market sessions](market-sessions.md): each instrument's exchange calendar (RTH, early closes, holidays) in the Markets detail's Session, Hours and Regular hours rows, and `market.get_sessions`.
- [Connections](connections.md): Settings › Connections and the status bar: each connection's state, adding, testing, editing and disconnecting a Rithmic login and a levels source from a spec on the sim's fakes, and where each exchange's data and orders go.
- [Late market](late-market.md): a desk opened before its market-data connection is added, and while it logs in, fills its charts, indicators, bid and ask, and Trade ticket read-out once the connection serves, with no reload.
- [Desk status](desk-status.md): the status line, DOM, footprint and command palette after a feed or runtime drop.
- [Paper orders](paper-orders.md): the Trade ticket's market orders, bracket (with its breakeven and trailing stop) and Flat on the Paper account, the orders Paper refuses without a live price, refusals in a pop-out chart window, orders dragged on the chart, the right-click that drops the order in hand, the margin and size limits a real futures account has, and the trade in the Journal.
- [Trading lock](trading-lock.md): an account locked to new entries for a span from its chip (5 min to 4 hours, the market's close or next open, the trading day, a New York, London or Tokyo session), the chip's `Locked until HH:MM`, the ticket's reason, the runtime's refusal of any entry (agent and copier too) while exits and stops work, the lock after a restart, and the typed early unlock.
- [Trade copier](trade-copier.md): a copy group with a leader and two followers, its entries, brackets, pause, resync and Flatten all.
- [Agent drafts](agent-drafts.md): an MCP client's draft order, its card and reply, the expired card, and the analyst's composer.
- [Analyst queue and steer](analyst-queue.md): Send beside Stop while the analyst works, a message steered into the running turn or queued (edited, removed, drained in order, held by Stop), and the queue a provider that can't steer falls back to, on stand-in CLIs.
- [Analyst sessions and New chat](analyst-sessions.md): the session menu's New chat when a model is ready, and the reason in its tooltip plus one action (turn on, or Model settings) when it is off, signed out or not installed; the shortcut behaves the same way.
- [Order flow](order-flow.md): the Scalp preset's footprint chart (figures that step down two sizes before the cells take over, Trades and Bid × Ask histogram modes, dimmed small rows and a max-level outline, the CVD as a histogram with delta % highlights and divergence marks, legend, drawing rail, volume profile by session, day, week, month or composite with volume, delta by price or both, a Market Profile (TPO) of letters or blocks from the same engine, fixed-range and anchored profiles on every timeframe, imbalance zones kept off the figures), big-trade bubbles (one fixed bubble per event at any zoom, Auto sized from the five sessions before, in three tiers, or a Fixed band, each bubble absorbed, pushed or neither, ten sessions on open), studies built from the stored days with the market closed, the legend's still placeholder while a study reads, and the DOM's column header.
- [Market closed](market-closed.md): on a weekend, a holiday or the halt every panel shows the last session, the chart and DOM say when the market opens again, the T&S lists the last prints once a fresh install has read them back, and nothing says stopped or no history.
- [Saved trades: scroll back](saved-trades-scrollback.md): saved days read before the connection is asked, older sessions pulled on scroll, and the big-trade model's five trailing sessions pulled on a fresh install; on the sim with `TESSERA_TEST_TRADES=replay` (the mock market otherwise keeps its own history), and in tests.
- [Saved trades](saved-trades.md): Settings › Data's saved trades (disk use, sessions kept, contracts), missing days downloading on their own (`Download missing data automatically`) with the coverage card showing their progress and why a day cannot be had, the date-range pull with its progress, cancel, resume and release, and the CSV export from the app and the CLI.
- [First week of history](first-week.md): the first run's offer after the login (the data stays here, each market's measured size and time, unknowns said), Download, the status bar's `History n/m days`, the pulls resuming after a restart, the filled week, and Settings › Data's `The last week`.
- [Replay](replay.md): a saved day played back on a chart from disk, its transport (play, speed, jump, back to live), and the orders it refuses.
- [A position on a busy tape](busy-position.md): with a position and its bracket open the desk keeps its frame rate on the busy market, says nothing is behind, shows Reverse and Flat as soon as the runtime takes them, keeps its P/L at two decimals so the read-out holds still, and shrinks a bracket's stop when its target fills in part.
- [Alerts](alerts.md): the alert form's order-flow subjects (a big trade or one print of at least a size, at the ask, the bid or either side) and a big-trade alert firing on the busy market, with its size, side and average price; the form's Pause and Delete, and the right-click menu on an alert's axis tag.
- [Sounds](sounds.md): the voice line each paper trade plays, what a lock and a rejected order say, and the lines that still play after the page's audio context is suspended, stalled or closed.
- [Usage and crash reports](usage-and-crash-reports.md): anonymous crash reports on from a fresh install (`sim.crash` before any answer), the first-run usage question with nothing chosen and Settings › App › Privacy's two switches, no usage before a Yes, nothing with both off (proved from the runtime's own sockets), runtime panics and window errors, the activation funnel's and each area's events, analyst traces, the masked session replay, logs, feature flags and the in-app survey (`scripts/usage-reports.ts`).
- [Stale numbers](stale-numbers.md): the position, P/L and prices that say "Runtime dropped", "2 min behind" or "No live prices" instead of looking live, the market orders that wait for live prices while Flat passes the gate, and the toast while a connection is down.
- [Research station](research-tester.md): every run with its idea, verdict and source chat, found by search, archived, restored or deleted; a run read at a glance (development against later, equity, quant stats, months, setup); each trade linked to its moment on the real chart and back.
- [Strategies between Research, the analyst and the journal](strategy-flow.md): a frozen study handed to the analyst's trading sessions (its signal read at each bar close, the analyst woken when it changes, its trades filed under a linked journal setup), and a journal setup opened back in Research as its study or a replay of its trades.
- [Feedback](feedback.md): the rail’s Feedback, the Tessera menu and ⌘⇧/, the sheet that shows every attached line (a picture with account numbers hidden, the app, the broker and account kind, the protect mode, the chart, the order and protect events, runtime errors) before anything goes, the connected agent’s write-up, and the report filed as a GitHub issue labelled feedback through Tessera Cloud, or queued offline and sent later (`scripts/feedback.ts`).
- [Sharing](share.md): Share chart… in a chart's menu, the dialog with the short link, the unlisted page that opens signed out with the Tessera mark and no account, and Settings › App › Shares where the owner deletes a link and it stops opening (`scripts/share.ts`).
- [Desktop updates](updates.md): the rail’s Check for updates above Feedback, the quiet card a found build shows (Later snoozes it, Update asks first), the restart confirmation with the working orders, open positions and analyst sessions it counts, the download that only Restart and update starts, and that download hidden: a bar under the rail’s item, reopened at the same percent, ready without a restart, picked up again after a reload; with the Tauri shell stood in for in the page.
