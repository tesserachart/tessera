# Desk charts

Each chart tile on the Desk shows one market at one timeframe. The user changes the timeframe from the tile's toolbar and the market from the tile's symbol button, and the workspace's layout in the runtime keeps both.

## Sub-features

- `chart-timeframe` sets the tile's timeframe from its `Timeframe` group: 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 1D.
- `chart-symbol` changes the tile's market through `Symbol search`.
- `chart-stored` keeps both in the workspace's layout in the runtime.
- `chart-panels` opens a DOM, time & sales or the alerts list from the chart, by mouse: the chart header's More menu and its right-click both list `Open DOM`, `Open Time & Sales` and `Show Alerts`: Open for a panel that shows the chart's own market, Show for one that is not about a market, as the palette's `Show <Title>` lines and a closed panel's faint `Show <Title>` tab say. The DOM and time & sales open beside that chart on its symbol (pinned, not linked); a second `Open DOM` on the same chart shows the one already open.
- `chart-wheel-live` the plain wheel zooms a chart that is following live around its right edge, so it keeps following and the last bar stays in view; scrolled back, or with Ctrl (a trackpad pinch), the wheel zooms around the pointer.
- `chart-print-bars` draws tick, range, volume, delta or renko bars built from the session's prints instead of time bars, starting again at each session's open (renko bricks run on across it), picked from `All intervals` (`Tick bars`, `Range bars`, `Volume bars`, `Delta bars`, `Renko`; tab-row text `500T`, `10R`, `1000V`, `300Δ`, `10Rk`). A delta bar closes on the print that takes its net delta to the size either way; a finished renko brick is exactly size ticks open to close with no wicks (only the forming brick, the last bar, shows the range since the last close), and a print that jumps several boxes leaves a brick for each, with no volume. `../scripts/delta-renko.ts <out> <label> [ui dir]` reads `market.get_print_bars` for 500T, 10R, 1000V, 300Δ and 10Rk and checks each finished renko brick is 10 ticks open to close with no wicks and brick times strictly rising, picks `300 delta` and `10 ticks renko` from `All intervals` on the NQ chart, reads `layout.get`, and saves stills of the menu and both charts, plus each chart zoomed in with the crosshair on a finished bar (`-hover`).
- `chart-any-interval` shows any interval the trader types in `All intervals`' `textbox "Interval"`: a count of seconds, minutes, hours or sessions (`15s`, `7m`, `90m`, `2h`, `2D`) or of ticks, range ticks or contracts (`377T`, `6R`, `2500V`), of delta (`300Δ`, `300 delta`) or of renko ticks (`8Rk`, `8 renko`). The field names what it read (`15 seconds`); Enter or `Show` puts the chart on it, and `Save` (or Ctrl/Cmd+Enter) also keeps it in the tab row, ticks included, in the runtime's `ui.preferences` `favouriteIntervals` so it is still there after a reload; text that is no interval says `Not an interval`. Seconds bars are built from the prints, minutes and hours from 1m, sessions from 1D. The agent sets the same intervals with `chart.set_timeframe` and reads them with `market.get_candles`. `../scripts/custom-interval.ts <out> <label> [ui dir]` types `15s`, `2D` and `377T`, saves `15s` and `377T` and reloads on `5m`, has the agent set `90m`, and saves each still with the runtime's answers.
- `chart-loading` a chart opened, or switched to another symbol, shows a skeleton of its own frame with no words (`status "Loading bars"`, read by screen readers only) until its history is in: the chart theme's grid, the price and time axes where the engine draws them (the price axis as wide as that symbol last needed), and placeholder candles, under the shared slow shimmer, which holds still under reduced motion. The bars land under it without the chart moving or remounting. It never holds back the desk around it, then draws the bars once: no candles appear one batch at a time, and the kept bars on disk are not drawn ahead of the fetched ones. If the read fails, the kept bars draw once and the load error shows in the same place. A chart switched to another timeframe of the same market draws it in the same frame, never showing `Loading bars`, when its bars are already held: 1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h and 4h, which a chart keeps loaded and live in the background once it has drawn. A switch to one not held (1D, any other interval) shows the pressed interval and the skeleton in the first frame after the click, with the old bars gone and the chart locked under it, then draws the new timeframe in one go. `../scripts/timeframe-switch.ts <out> <label> [ui dir]` switches the NQ chart 1m → 5m → 15m with every connection read held 2 s, logs how many frames showed `Loading bars`, and records `<label>.webm`. `../scripts/switch-feedback.ts <out> <label> [ui dir]` does 5m → 1D → 15m → 5m with big trades on the busy market, reads held 2 s, and logs per switch the first frame after the click: which interval is pressed and whether the skeleton shows. A chart that has drawn never shows it again on a reload or a reconnect, and older pages scrolled into view land in one piece. The footer names its bars and connection once they are in, and says nothing before. `../scripts/held-reads.ts` stills it as `<scene>-bars-loading.png`.
- `chart-reconnect-fill` closes the hole a market-data drop leaves: when the feed comes back, the runtime reads the bars from the chart's last bar to now and sends them before the live ones, so the candles run on without a gap or a doubled bar; the footprint's closed bars for the hole follow, and big trades read the hole from history once the trades feed restarts (`TradesUpdate.segment`). The frozen sim's feed never drops its streams, so `crates/tessera-market/tests/hub.rs` (`a_feed_that_drops_and_comes_back…`) proves it on a feed that does.
- `chart-hours` shows which hours the chart draws in a pill in its header, `button "Chart hours: Extended hours"` (`● ETH`, every hour, the default) or `Chart hours: Regular hours` (`● RTH`); the dot is lit while those hours trade. Its menu, `menuitemradio "Regular hours"` and `"Extended hours"`, each with its hours in the market's zone, sets them for that chart only, kept as `charts[…].hours` `rth` (absent for ETH). On RTH the chart asks `market.get_candles` with `session: "rth"`, so the overnight bars leave the chart and its time axis, and every study follows: indicator series and CVD are measured over the RTH bars (`indicators.series` `session: "rth"`), and a volume profile on its default hours, `Chart` (its select reads `Chart · RTH`) draws RTH sessions and RTH composites. A profile set to its own hours keeps them, and its legend names them (`VP ETH`). The pill is not shown on a market with no regular hours or on tick, range or volume bars. The agent sets the same with `chart.set_hours`. `../scripts/chart-hours.ts <out> <label> [ui dir]` adds a 5-session composite to the NQ 5m chart, switches the pill to RTH, checks the drawn composite's volume (`chart.get_state` `viewport.profiles`) against `market.get_volume_profile` composite `session: "rth"` (1,828,025 against 2,716,333 for all hours on the sim), sets it back with `chart.set_hours`, and saves stills of each.
- `chart-history-refused` a chart whose feed refuses older history (Rithmic's `request rejected: [13] permission denied`) keeps every bar it has and shows no centred error: a quiet dashed line at its first bar reads `Older history isn’t available on this login`, and neither the refused read nor older pages are asked again. Only a chart with no bars at all says `<symbol> did not load. Not available on this login. Ask your broker about access.` The runtime's bar cache answers a refusal met while reaching further back with the bars it already read, and its background fill stops there (`crates/tessera-market/tests/history.rs`, `older_bars_refused…`, `the_fill_stops_where_the_connection_refuses…`). `node .agents/skills/verify-tessera/scripts/refused-history.ts [evidence dir] [ui dir] [pr|main]` starts its own sim runtime three times: a reopened desk with every read refused (the kept bars draw, no centred error, one ask per chart), older pages refused as the NQ chart is dragged back to its first bar (the mark, one ask), and a cold desk with every read refused (the centred error, one ask per chart). It saves `<scene>-reopened`, `<scene>-older-refused` and `<scene>-first-load-refused` with what was asked.
- `chart-viewport` honours a view the agent asks for with `chart.set_viewport`, or says why not. `lastBars` shows exactly that many bars and `fitAll` every bar held, fitted right of the drawing toolbar's band; `from` starts at the bar that spans it (a 1D bar opens the evening before its day). The chart reads older pages (tick, range and volume bars: one more session at a time, up to 5) until it holds what was asked; when history stops short, a quiet note on the chart and the tool's `shown.short` say so (`477 of 700 bars: no older history`, `Nothing from Oct 22 yet: the last bar is Oct 2`). The asked view holds through data, resizes, tab and workspace switches and remounts until the user pans, zooms or presses a key. `shown` is the view the chart answered with (absent when no desk draws the chart, or it did not answer in 8 s); `chart.get_state`'s `viewport` keeps `short` and `follows`. `../scripts/viewport-range.ts <out> <label> [ui dir]` asks NQ 1h for 700 bars, switches a tab and a workspace away and back, asks 1000V for 900, 5m for fitAll, ES 1D from Sep 22 and from Oct 22, then hides NQ's price, and saves each answer and still.
- `chart-no-price` hides one chart's price series: `Chart type` › `No price` (the toolbar's `button "Chart type: …"`, the chart menu and the right-click), kept as `charts[…].chartType` `none`; its price axis, studies and drawings stay, other charts keep theirs, and any other type shows the price again. The agent does the same with `chart.set_chart_type` `none`. A new chart's default type (Settings) never offers it.
- `chart-front-months` draws a 1D chart of a dated equity-index future through its earlier quarterlies, each from its roll date, unadjusted.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- The tile's `toolbar "Chart"`, `group "Timeframe"`.
- The tile's symbol button, `button "<symbol>: change symbol"`, which opens `dialog "Symbol search"`.
- The chart header's `button "Chart: more"` (the ⋯ at the end of its tab row) and a right-click on the chart.

## Driving it with the e2e MCP

Preconditions:

- The baseline in `README.md`.

- **Timeframe.** In `region "NQ Z6 5m"`, tap `button "15m"` of `group "Timeframe"`. The region is renamed `region "NQ Z6 15m"` and its `button "15m"` is `[pressed]`.
- **Symbol.** Tap `button "NQ Z6: change symbol"`. `dialog "Symbol search"` opens with `combobox "Search symbols"` and options such as `option "ES Z6 E-mini S&P 500 Dec 2026 5,583.25 +1.14%"`. Tap that option. The region is renamed `region "ES Z6 15m"`.
- **Stored.** Run `runtime.ts <port> layout.get '{"layoutId":"default"}'`. `charts["chart-1"]` has `instrument` `ESZ6.CME` and `timeframe` `15m`, and `charts["chart-2"]` is unchanged (`ESZ6.CME`, `1m`).
- **Panels from the chart.** On the ES chart's header (`region "ES Z6 1m"`), tap `button "Chart: more"`, then `menuitem "Open DOM"`. `region "ES Z6 · DOM"` opens beside the ES chart, its ladder around ES Z6 5,583.25. Open the menu again and tap `menuitem "Open Time & Sales"`, then `menuitem "Show Alerts"`: `region "ES Z6 · T&S"` opens under the DOM, and the bottom strip shows its `Alerts` tab. Run `runtime.ts <port> layout.get '{"layoutId":"default"}'` after the dock saves: the dock holds a `dom` and a `tape` panel whose params have `instrument` `ESZ6.CME` and `link` `null`. A right-click on the chart lists the same three items above `Chart type`; the e2e engine's right-click does not reach the chart's canvas, so check that one with Playwright's `mouse.click(…, { button: 'right' })`.
- **Front months.** In `region "ES Z6 1m"`, tap `button "1D"`. The tile's `toolbar "Layers"` reads `357 bars · Mock market` and `Front months from ES M5, unadjusted`. Hover a bar in early April 2026: the legend's second row starts with `ES M6`. Run `runtime.ts <port> market.get_candles '{"instrument":"ESZ6.CME","tf":"1D","limit":1500,"frontMonths":true}'`: `contracts` runs from `ESM5.CME` to `ESZ6.CME`, `ESZ6.CME` from 2026-09-10 and `ESU6.CME` from 2026-06-10. Without `frontMonths` the answer has no `contracts`.

- **Wheel on a live chart.** The e2e `scroll` verb cannot reach the canvas under its overlay, so use Playwright's `mouse.wheel` over the middle of the plot. On the frozen clock nothing ticks, so read "following" from the view: after a few notches up the last bar keeps its place near the price axis, the same right margin in bars as before the zoom. Press `ArrowLeft` once and wheel again: the bar under the pointer stays under it. The busy market (`docs/dev.md` §9) shows it live: new bars keep arriving at the right edge after the zoom.
- **Any interval.** In `region "NQ Z6 1m"`, tap `button "All intervals"`, type `15s` into `textbox "Interval"` (it shows `15 seconds`) and press Enter. The region is renamed `region "NQ Z6 15s"`, the `Timeframe` group shows `button "15s"` `[pressed]`, and `layout.get` gives `charts["chart-1"].timeframe` `15s`. `runtime.ts <port> market.get_candles '{"instrument":"NQ","tf":"15s","limit":200}'` answers 200 bars, each opening on a 15-second boundary. Typed `2D` draws one candle per two sessions; typed `377T` draws 377-print bars. Type `15s` and tap `Save`, then tap `button "5m"` and reload: the `Timeframe` group still holds `button "15s"`, and `runtime.ts <port> settings.get '{"keys":["ui.preferences"]}'` lists `15s` in `favouriteIntervals`.
- **Tick, range and volume bars.** In `region "NQ Z6 5m"`, tap `button "All intervals"` and pick `menuitemradio "500 ticks"` under `Tick bars`. The region is renamed `region "NQ Z6 500T"`, the `Timeframe` group shows `button "500T"` `[pressed]`, and the chart draws bars of 500 prints each. `layout.get` gives `charts["chart-1"].printBars` `{"kind":"tick","size":500}`; `runtime.ts <port> market.get_print_bars '{"instrument":"NQ","bars":{"kind":"tick","size":500}}'` answers the same bars, each `v` the sum of 500 prints' sizes. Tap `button "5m"`: the tile is back on time bars and `printBars` is gone.

## Gotchas

- After the symbol change two tiles show ES Z6 (`ES Z6 15m` and `ES Z6 1m`). Tell them apart by timeframe, or by `chart-1` and `chart-2` in the layout.
- `Symbol search` lists RTY, YM, CL and GC as "No connection serves it": the mock market serves NQ, ES, MNQ and MES among the futures.
- The frozen market never ticks, so a chart's last bar never changes.
- The mock's expired quarterlies serve daily bars only and take no orders. On the mock their prices repeat the front contract's, so only the legend tag tells them apart.
